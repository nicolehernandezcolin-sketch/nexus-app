Help and contact

We answer within one business day.

Email: support@nexusglobal.info

For a ticket problem, include the event name and the email on your Nexus account.

Common questions

I didn’t get my confirmation or password email

Check spam and promotions. On the sign-in screen, tap “Send it again” or “Forgot password?”. Links expire after an hour.

Where are my tickets?

Events → My tickets. Each ticket shows its door code. If you paid and don’t see them after a few minutes, email us.

Can I get a refund?

Cancelled events are refunded in full, including the fee, without you needing to ask. Postponed events: ask within 30 days of the change. See Tickets and refunds.

Someone is harassing me or posted my work

Tap ••• on the post, profile, message or group and choose Report or Block. We review every report within 24 hours. For anything urgent or unsafe, also contact local authorities.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → Delete my account. It’s permanent.

Accessibility

Nexus supports VoiceOver, larger text sizes and Reduce Motion. If something is hard to use with assistive technology, tell us at the email above and we’ll fix it.
