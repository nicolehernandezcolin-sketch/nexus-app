Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

The short version: we collect what Nexus needs to work, show it only to the people you choose, never sell it, never use it to train AI, and don’t use ads or tracking. You can delete everything by deleting your account.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and password (stored scrambled; we can’t read it).
  • Profile: what you add: photo, bio, location, roles, skills, projects, prompt answers, header style, and your settings.
  • What you share: posts and photos, private group posts, private messages and their photos, follows, saves and collaboration requests.
  • Events: RSVPs, tickets you buy, and when your ticket is checked in at the door.
  • Payments: you pay on Stripe’s page. Stripe handles your card; Nexus never sees or stores card numbers. We keep a record of each order (event, ticket type, quantity, amount and status).
  • Reports and blocks you make, so we can act on them.
  • Your location on the shows map: only if you tap “Show me on the map” and allow it. It stays on your phone to put you on the map; Nexus doesn’t receive or store it.
  • Technical: our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of a request) for security, for a limited time.

We don’t use advertising, analytics trackers, or third-party SDKs that track you across other apps and websites.

How we use it

  • To run Nexus: your profile, timeline, Discover (only if you switch it on), groups, messages, events and tickets.
  • To send account emails: confirming your email and resetting your password.
  • To keep Nexus safe: reviewing reports, enforcing ticket limits, and preventing fraud.
  • To meet legal duties, such as tax and payment records.

Never: we don’t sell or rent your information, we don’t share it for advertising, and we don’t use your work, or let anyone use it, to train AI.

Who can see what

  • Nothing on Nexus is visible to people who aren’t signed in.
  • Members can see open posts, profiles and events. Discover lists you only if you turn it on.
  • Private group posts and files: only that group’s members. Private messages: only the two of you.
  • Event hosts see the name on each ticket and its check-in status for their own events.
  • Photos load through private links that expire after an hour.

Private messages and group posts are encrypted in transit and at rest and locked to their members. They are not end-to-end encrypted, so Nexus staff could technically access them, which we only do to investigate a report or when the law requires it.

Companies that help run Nexus

They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in (servers in the USA)
  • Stripe: ticket payments (Stripe’s privacy policy)
  • Resend: account emails
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the website version and these pages
  • Apple Maps (iPhone) and OpenStreetMap (website): the shows map. Loading the map sends the area being viewed, not your account.

We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your information while you have an account. Delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete my account) and we delete your profile, posts, photos, messages, groups you own, follows and settings right away. Order and ticket records are kept, without your name attached, for refunds, taxes and accounting. Server logs and backups roll off on their own schedule.

Your rights

You can see and edit your information in the app, delete it, or email us for a copy of it. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA to know, correct and delete personal information and to not be discriminated against for using them. We don’t sell or share personal information as those laws define it. To make a request, email us.

Children

Nexus is not for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
